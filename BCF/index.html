Core Operational Focus

The Four Action Pillars of BCF

Structured civic interventions designed for maximum ecological impact and accessible public participation.

Pillar 01 • Clean

Lake & Waterbody Cleaning

Restoring Bhoj wetland and city water reservoirs by clearing non-biodegradable plastics, discarded fishing nylon, and hazardous shoreline debris before it breaks into microplastics.

Cadence: Every 1st & 3rd Sunday morning (7:00 AM – 9:30 AM).
Gear: Heavy-duty nitrile gloves, jute waste bags, litter grabbers provided.
Disposal: Segregation into dry recyclables & municipal tie-up with BMC Bhopal.
Pillar 02 • Plant

Indigenous Miyawaki Afforestation

Creating dense micro-forests using the proven Akira Miyawaki method. We select climate-resilient native species that restore subterranean groundwater and shelter local birdlife.

Species: Neem, Peepal, Bargad, Karanj, Amaltas, Jamun, Sheesham.
Method: Soil perforation, rice husk aerators, cow dung compost enrichment.
Care Protocol: Assigned volunteer buddy system ensuring 3-year watering follow-ups.
Pillar 03 • Care

Ridge Seed Ball Dispersal

Greening inaccessible rocky terrain around Manuabhan Tekri, Kaliyasot slopes, and peripheral barren ridges using nutrient-coated seed balls rolled by community volunteers and school children.

Composition: 5 parts red clay, 3 parts vermicompost, 1 part native hard seeds.
Timing: Dispersed 2 weeks prior to South-West monsoon arrival.
Success Rate: Protected from birds and rodents until first monsoon rain triggers sprouting.
Pillar 04 • Upharam

Project Upharam (Joy of Giving)

Civic care extends beyond nature to human dignity. Project Upharam collects, sanitizes, and distributes winter clothing, school kits, and storybooks to underprivileged children across informal settlements.

Impact: 1,100+ articles distributed across 8 government schools & bastis.
Donation Hubs: Collection kiosks stationed in Arera Colony & MP Nagar.
Process: Volunteers wash, iron, pack, and directly hand over with zero middlemen.
