What the Numbers Mean

Beyond the Statistics

Each number represents a human story — a breakthrough, a skill earned, a family transformed.

🎓

Skill-Building That Lasts

Our 500+ trained participants did not just learn a trade — they gained confidence, independence, and a sustainable livelihood path. Each graduate leaves with a nationally recognised skill certificate and hands-on production experience.

💚

Women at the Centre

Of our trained participants, over 300 are women from marginalised or economically vulnerable backgrounds. Our gender-sensitive approach ensures women feel safe, supported, and celebrated throughout their journey.

🏘️

Community Ripple Effect

When one woman earns a stable income, an average of 4 family members benefit. Across our 10+ communities, improved earning capacity has translated to better nutrition, school enrolment, and reduced domestic stress.
