Our Curriculum

All Training Programs

Six comprehensive programs covering every stage of garment creation — from needle and thread to enterprise and entrepreneurship.

🧵

Sewing & Tailoring

⏱ [X] Weeks 🌱 Beginner

The foundation of all garment skills. Participants learn the fundamentals of hand and machine sewing, progressing through basic stitching to full garment construction and alteration techniques. Ideal for complete beginners with no prior experience.

Curriculum Highlights

  • Basic hand stitching techniques and stitch types
  • Introduction to machine sewing and needle work
  • Garment assembly — joining, seaming, hemming
  • Alterations, repairs, and finishing

Machine Operation

⏱ [X] Weeks 🌱 Beginner–Intermediate

A focused program on mastering the sewing machine — from understanding its parts to high-speed precision operation. Participants learn threading, tension settings, speed control, and routine maintenance to ensure consistent quality output.

Curriculum Highlights

  • Machine parts identification and functions
  • Threading, bobbin winding, and tension setup
  • Speed control and precision stitching practice
  • Basic maintenance and troubleshooting

Garment Making

⏱ [X] Weeks 📈 Intermediate

The complete garment-making journey from body measurements to finished product. This intermediate program teaches participants how to plan, cut, assemble, and complete a full garment with professional standards and quality finishing.

Curriculum Highlights

  • Taking accurate body measurements
  • Introduction to patterns and drafting basics
  • Fabric cutting, layout, and preparation
  • Full garment construction and finishing

Pattern & Cutting

⏱ [X] Weeks 📈 Intermediate

Precision is at the heart of professional garment making. This program trains participants to read and interpret commercial patterns, mark fabric accurately, and execute clean cuts for consistent, waste-minimal production.

Curriculum Highlights

  • Reading and interpreting pattern instructions
  • Fabric marking using chalk and tracing tools
  • Precision cutting techniques and safety
  • Efficient layout planning to minimise waste

🌸

Embroidery & Decorative

⏱ [X] Weeks 🎨 All Levels

Add value and artistry to garments with decorative embroidery skills. This program covers both hand embroidery traditions and modern machine embroidery, enabling participants to produce higher-value, distinctively finished products.

Curriculum Highlights

  • Thread types, colours, and fabric compatibility
  • Traditional embroidery patterns and techniques
  • Machine embroidery settings and design
  • Quality finishing and presentation

Entrepreneurship & Livelihood

⏱ [X] Weeks 🚀 Advanced

Transform skills into sustainable income. This program equips skilled participants with the knowledge to start and grow a small garment business — covering pricing, customer relations, basic accounting, and community marketing strategies.

Curriculum Highlights

  • Product pricing and costing basics
  • Customer handling and service skills
  • Basic accounting and expense tracking
  • Local marketing and community outreach
