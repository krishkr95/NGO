How We Bridge Learning & Earning

A Living Production Unit Powered by Trained Artisans

Unlike standard training centers where practice fabric is discarded, our center operates an active garment and textile production line.

Here, trainees who complete their foundational courses transition directly into structured production batches. They gain real-world industrial stitching speed, precision quality control experience, and earn fair wages for every garment crafted.

✓
Community Made: 100% of our products are stitched by trained local women and artisans.
✓
Commercial Quality: Strict 4-point inspection ensures seam durability, sizing accuracy, and clean finishing.
✓
Social Impact Purchasing: Every uniform or bag ordered directly sustains livelihood wages for artisan families.

Our Manufacturing Pipeline

The 7-Step Production Process

From premium raw fabric to finished, packaged garments — engineered for high quality and minimal environmental waste.

01
📦

1. Fabric Sourcing

High-grade cotton, drill, polyester-cotton blends, and canvas tested for color fastness and shrinkage.

02
📐

2. Measurement

Standardized grading across child and adult size charts with custom dimensional adjustments for institutional orders.

03
✂️

3. Precision Cutting

Multi-layer fabric spreading and precision shear cutting along marked grainlines with zero fabric distortion.

04
⚙️

4. Machine Stitching

Assembled on industrial lockstitch and overlock sewing machines with reinforced tension points and durable thread.

05
✨

5. Finishing & Buttons

Automatic buttonhole stitching, button attachments, edge trimming, and industrial steam iron pressing.

06
🔍

6. Quality Audit

100% individual inspection for dimensional accuracy, seam strength, loose threads, and visual symmetry.

07
🎁

7. Packaging & Client Delivery

Eco-friendly folded packaging, batch labeling, and timely delivery to schools, NGOs, hospitals, and corporate CSR clients.

Infrastructure

Our Production Capabilities & Machines

Equipped with commercial garment equipment to fulfill small custom batches as well as large institutional orders.

🪡

Industrial Lockstitch

Direct-drive motorized single needle machines for high-speed, consistent straight stitching.

🧵

4-Thread Overlock

High-speed edge trimming and safety overlock seams preventing fraying on woven & knit apparel.

🔘

Buttonhole & Button Attach

Automated machines for uniform shirt buttonholes and secure 4-hole button fastening.

💨

Industrial Steam Press

Vacuum steam ironing stations giving garments crisp creases and ready-to-wear presentation.
