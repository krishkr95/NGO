Corporate Social Responsibility

Partner With Us to Create Sustainable Impact

Align your corporate CSR mandates with impactful, women-led vocational skill development and transparent social governance.

⚙️

Sewing Machine Grants

Sponsor industrial lockstitch or overlock machines for new community training centers in rural and semi-urban clusters.

👩‍🎓

Batch Training Sponsorship

Cover 3-month comprehensive vocational training and materials for a cohort of 25 to 50 underprivileged women.

🛍️

Ethical CSR Procurement

Source company event bags, merchandise, corporate gifts, and uniform requirements through our community artisan unit.

School Uniform Drives

Support government school students by funding uniform sets stitched locally by community mothers.
