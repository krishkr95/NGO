Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about visiting and connecting.

Walk-ins for training admissions are welcome Monday through Saturday between 10:00 AM and 5:00 PM. For CSR delegations or bulk garment order discussions, we recommend scheduling a prior time slot via WhatsApp.
During regular working hours (10:00 AM – 6:00 PM, Monday to Saturday), our coordinator typically responds within 15–30 minutes. Messages sent on Sundays or holidays are addressed the next working morning.
