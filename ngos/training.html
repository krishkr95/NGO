Curriculum Focus

What Participants Learn & Master

Our hands-on training builds practical competency across every aspect of machine sewing, fabric craftsmanship, and finished garment assembly.

⚙️

1. Machine Operation

Threading, bobbin winding, motor foot-pedal speed control, needle replacement, and troubleshooting common stitch jams.

🧵

2. Fabric Handling

Understanding cotton, linen, silk, synthetic blends, fabric grain, tension adjustments, and pre-shrink washing.

📏

3. Body Measurement

Taking accurate human anatomical measurements, calculating ease allowances, and recording tailored specification charts.

✂️

4. Pattern & Cutting

Drafting patterns on cardstock, chalk marking onto fabric, grainline alignment, and precision rotary and shear cutting.

🪡

5. Stitching & Seams

Straight stitch, zig-zag, overlock safety seams, French seams, rolled hems, darts, pleats, and gathers.

👗

6. Garment Assembly

Joining bodices, attaching sleeves, stitching collars, inserting zippers, sewing plackets, and button attachments.

👔

7. Finishing & Pressing

Thread trimming, steam pressing, collar shaping, buttonhole punching, and applying clean interior bias tape.

🔍

8. Quality Checking

Tolerance checks against measurement charts, seam strength testing, symmetrical alignment, and defect identification.

🔧

9. Basic Maintenance

Routine machine oiling, lint cleaning in bobbin cases, belt tension adjustments, and safety compliance.

Learning Journey

The 6-Step Training Pathway

How a participant progresses from complete beginner to certified, earning garment artisan.

01

Orientation & Safety

Introduction to center rules, workplace safety around electrical sewing machines, ergonomic posture, needle safety, and training expectations.

02

Machine Familiarisation & Paper Stitching

Practicing unthreaded straight lines and curves on paper sheets to build foot pedal speed coordination and needle control.

03

Practical Fabric Stitching & Seams

Threaded machine stitching on scrap fabric, mastering parallel seams, corner turning, piping, edge overlocking, and hem folds.

04

Garment Production & Assembly

Constructing full garments from scratch: school uniforms, kurtis, blouses, skirts, and bags under close instructor supervision.

05

Quality Control & Finishing

Measuring tolerances, thread trimming, industrial steam ironing, button finishing, and packaging garments for client dispatch.

06

Livelihood, Enterprise & Placements

Graduation certificate award, guidance on microcredit machine purchase, joining production cooperatives, or starting home tailoring businesses.
